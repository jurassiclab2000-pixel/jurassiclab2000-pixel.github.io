Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026

Welcome to CopyMe. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the CopyMe mobile application for English listening and shadowing practice.

1. Information We Collect

Information handled by CopyMe: CopyMe provides built-in learning content. It does not require an account, does not record your voice, and does not send your shadowing practice or learning progress to our servers. App settings and any locally maintained progress are stored on your device.

Information handled by advertising services: CopyMe uses Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information, such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • device or advertising identifiers;
  • information about interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps, or ad views; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

The information processed by AdMob depends on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, applicable consent choices, and Google's policies. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information in advertising.

2. How Information Is Used

CopyMe uses locally stored settings and progress to support the app's learning features. AdMob may process information for purposes such as serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud and abuse, and improving advertising services, in accordance with Google's policies and the applicable settings and consent choices.

3. Local Storage and Audio

CopyMe's learning content is built into the app. The app does not record your voice for shadowing practice. App settings and locally maintained progress remain on your device and are not uploaded by CopyMe to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

CopyMe uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. AdMob may collect and share information as described above and in Google's own policies. We do not control Google's independent data practices; please review Google's policies for further details.

5. Advertising Choices

The type of ads shown may depend on AdMob configuration, applicable consent requirements, and your device or account settings. We have not made a promise that all ads will be personalized or that all ads will be non-personalized. You can review or adjust available advertising and privacy controls in your device settings and, where offered, through the consent options presented in the app.

6. Data Security

CopyMe keeps app settings and locally maintained progress on your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information handled by AdMob is subject to Google's security practices and policies.

7. Children's Privacy

CopyMe is intended for general English learners, primarily teenagers and adults, and is not specifically designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. AdMob's data practices and the requirements that apply to an app can vary by audience, region, and configuration. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information processed about you, including rights to access or delete certain information or to object to certain processing. For information collected or processed by Google through AdMob, please refer to Google's privacy tools and policies. You may also contact us with questions about CopyMe's handling of information.
