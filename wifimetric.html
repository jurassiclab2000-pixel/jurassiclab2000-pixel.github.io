Permissions & Privacy 權限與隱私

WiFi Metric requires two system permissions. Here's exactly why — no hidden data collection. WiFi Metric 需要兩項系統權限。以下說明確切原因，不含隱藏資料蒐集。

  • 📍
    ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Android requires location permission for any app that scans Wi-Fi networks (since Android 9). WiFi Metric uses this solely to enable the scan API — your physical location is never stored or transmitted. Android 9 起，所有執行 WiFi 掃描的 App 都必須持有定位權限。WiFi Metric 僅用此權限呼叫掃描 API，不儲存或傳輸您的實際位置。
  • 📶
    ACCESS_WIFI_STATE / CHANGE_WIFI_STATE Needed to read scan results and trigger manual rescans on demand. 用於讀取掃描結果並在使用者需要時觸發手動重新掃描。
