Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Welcome to Gaia Scout. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Gaia Scout mobile application to explore and watch nature and wildlife livestreams from around the world.

1. Information We Collect

Information handled by Gaia Scout: Gaia Scout does not require you to create an account or sign in. The app does not directly collect personal information, record your voice, or require you to provide personal details to watch livestreams.

Gaia Scout retrieves a list of publicly available livestream URLs from Cloudflare R2, a cloud storage service. This list is stored locally on your device to support browsing and accessing livestreams. Gaia Scout does not upload this locally stored URL list or your viewing activity to our own servers.

Information handled by Cloudflare: When the app retrieves livestream URL lists from Cloudflare R2, Cloudflare may process certain technical information associated with these requests, such as IP addresses, device or network information, and request logs, as necessary to provide and secure its services.

For more information, please review Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Information handled by YouTube: Gaia Scout uses an embedded YouTube Player to play livestream videos directly within the app. When you watch a video, YouTube or Google may collect or process certain information, such as:

  • IP address and general location information;
  • device information and identifiers;
  • video playback activity and interactions with the YouTube Player; and
  • diagnostic, performance, and usage information.

The information processed by YouTube depends on its services, technologies, device settings, and applicable privacy choices. Gaia Scout does not control YouTube's independent data collection practices.

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and YouTube's Terms of Service.

Information handled by advertising services: Gaia Scout uses Google AdMob to display ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information, such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
  • device or advertising identifiers;
  • information about interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps, or ad views; and
  • diagnostic and performance information.

The information processed by AdMob depends on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, applicable consent choices, and Google's policies. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information in advertising.

2. How Information Is Used

Gaia Scout uses the livestream URL list retrieved from Cloudflare R2 to provide access to publicly available nature and wildlife livestreams.

The URL list is stored locally on your device to support app functionality and reduce the need to repeatedly retrieve the same information.

Cloudflare may process technical request information to deliver and secure its storage services. YouTube may process information to provide video playback and related services. AdMob may process information for purposes such as serving and measuring ads, preventing fraud and abuse, and improving advertising services, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable settings and consent choices.

3. Local Storage and Video Playback

Gaia Scout does not require an account or login. The app retrieves livestream URL information from Cloudflare R2 and stores that information locally on your device.

The locally stored information consists of livestream URLs and related app data needed to provide the app's features. This information is not uploaded by Gaia Scout to our own servers.

Livestream videos are played through the embedded YouTube Player. Video playback is provided by YouTube, and YouTube or Google may process information as described in their own privacy policies.

Gaia Scout does not record your voice, access your microphone for recording, or upload audio recordings to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

Gaia Scout uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare R2: Used to store and provide livestream URL lists to the app. Cloudflare may process technical information associated with requests to its services.
  • YouTube: Used to provide and play publicly available livestream videos through an embedded YouTube Player.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements within the app.

These third-party services may independently collect or process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their independent data practices. Please review their respective policies for further details.

5. Advertising Choices

The type of ads shown may depend on AdMob configuration, applicable consent requirements, and your device or account settings. We have not made a promise that all ads will be personalized or that all ads will be non-personalized.

You can review or adjust available advertising and privacy controls in your device settings and, where offered, through the consent options presented in the app.

6. Data Security

Gaia Scout stores its livestream URL list and related local app data on your device. We do not maintain user accounts or store your viewing history on our own servers.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Information handled by Cloudflare, YouTube, and Google AdMob is subject to their respective security practices and policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Gaia Scout is intended for general audiences interested in nature, wildlife, and exploring live scenes around the world. The app is not specifically designed for children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. However, third-party services such as YouTube and Google AdMob may process information according to their own policies and applicable requirements.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information processed about you, including rights to access or delete certain information or to object to certain processing.

For information collected or processed by Cloudflare, YouTube, or Google AdMob, please refer to their respective privacy policies and privacy tools.

You may also contact us with questions about Gaia Scout's handling of information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated when appropriate. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
